Privacy notice

We don't collect your answers

Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short: the PDPA Readiness Check runs entirely in your browser. Your organisation profile, answers and notes are never sent to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising trackers and no cookies.

What is stored, and where

Your answers are saved in your own browser's local storage so you can come back to the assessment later. They stay on your device. You can delete them at any time with "Start over" on the results page, or by clearing your browser's site data.

What the website loads

The page is served by our hosting provider (Vercel), which, like any web server, processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the page and protect against abuse. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the fonts are fetched. Neither receives your assessment answers.

Exports

When you copy the report or action plan, the text goes to your clipboard only. Where you paste or share it is up to you.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you from this tool, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question about this notice, open an issue on the project's GitHub page.

Changes

If the tool ever starts collecting data, this notice will be updated before that happens.
