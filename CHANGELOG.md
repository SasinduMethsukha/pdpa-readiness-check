# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-20)
First public release.

- 105-question bank mapped to the Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022, as amended by Act No. 22 of 2025
- 13 profile questions that switch off items that don't apply
- Quick check (~10 minutes) and full audit modes
- Public-sector items (s20(1)(a), s26(4)–(5), DPA Circular 01/2026)
- Weighted scoring: headline score for obligations in force from 1 January 2027; Parts II and IV scored separately
- Step-by-step remediation for every question; 30/60/90-day roadmap
- Printable report with executive summary, critical findings, roadmap and sign-off; Markdown and CSV exports
- Responsive layout with bottom navigation on phones
- Privacy notice, terms, MIT licence (code) and CC BY 4.0 (question bank)
- Question bank reviewed by an external reviewer (8 new questions, 6 revisions, 3 scoping questions)
