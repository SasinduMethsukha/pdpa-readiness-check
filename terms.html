Accuracy

The question bank is based on the Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022, as amended by Act No. 22 of 2025, and on publicly available DPA material at the time of the last update. The Authority continues to issue rules, directives and guidelines, and some items (for example the 72-hour breach notification target) come from draft rules. Scores, weights and recommendations are a structured opinion, not a legal determination. Check dpa.gov.lk and take professional advice before relying on the results.
