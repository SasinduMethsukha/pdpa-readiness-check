About this check

Every question maps to a section of the Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022, as amended by Act No. 22 of 2025. Yes = 1, Partly = 0.5, No and Not sure = 0. N/A is excluded. Items are weighted 3 (critical), 2 (important) or 1 (good practice). The headline score covers obligations in force from 1 January 2027 (Parts I and III). Parts II and IV are scored separately.

This is a self-assessment aid, not legal advice. The DPA is still issuing rules and guidelines; the 72-hour breach target comes from its draft rules. Answers stay in your browser and are never sent anywhere.
